🌍 **2050: Letters from the Future** 
is a story-driven 2D environmental game by **TENSOR TITANS**, inspired by the United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 13: Climate Action. When a teenager receives a mysterious letter from Jennifer, a girl living in the year 2050, he discovers a future where the river beside his home has disappeared—a river filled with his childhood memories and precious moments with his siblings. Determined to change this heartbreaking future, he embarks on a mission to restore the environment through interactive challenges involving river cleaning, unclogging drains, planting trees, waste segregation, and sustainable transportation. Every mission brings him closer to uncovering the truth and unlocking Jennifer’s next letter, revealing how the choices made today can shape the world of tomorrow. Blending mystery, emotional storytelling, and environmental awareness, the game reminds players that climate action begins with everyday decisions. **The future is not written yet—and every choice matters.** 🌱
